METHOD

Culture is noisy. Evidence should not be.

We preserve the path from original observation to cultural interpretation so every signal can be inspected rather than simply believed.

01 SOURCES
Publications, creators, street observation, newsletters, video, communities
→
02 CONTENT
Articles, posts, videos, interviews, images
→
03 STRUCTURE
People, brands, objects, places, scenes
→
04 SIGNALS
Recurring cultural patterns
→
05 CONFIRMATION
Independent evidence across sources and source types
→
06 INTELLIGENCE
What's forming, where, who carries it, how early
Independence matters

Ten posts from one publication are ten observations, but still only one source. Multiple channels from the same organization do not count as independent confirmation.

Source diversity

We combine different types of cultural evidence rather than treating one platform as reality.

Provenance

Every observation retains its original source, date and context.

Independent confirmation

Repeated content from one organization does not create artificial confidence.

Confidence

Signals carry visible confidence based on evidence strength, diversity and recency.

Editorial judgment

Automation helps detect patterns. Cultural interpretation remains explicit, editable and open to contradiction.

Public digital behavior is evidence, not a complete representation of a population or city.
