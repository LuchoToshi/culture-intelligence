METHOD

Culture is noisy. Evidence should not be.

We preserve the path from original observation to cultural interpretation so every signal can be inspected rather than simply believed. This page explains how the intelligence is made — and, just as importantly, what it cannot claim.

From observation to intelligence

01 SOURCESPublications, creators, street observation, newsletters, video, communities. Different types of cultural evidence, never one platform treated as reality.
02 CONTENTArticles, posts, videos, interviews, images — collected continuously and stored with their original source, date and context.
03 STRUCTUREEach item is analyzed into people, brands, objects, places, scenes and themes, with facts kept strictly separate from interpretation.
04 SIGNALSRecurring cultural patterns across items become provisional signals. One observation is never a signal on its own.
05 CONFIRMATIONA signal strengthens only through independent evidence — different sources, and different types of source.
06 INTELLIGENCEWhat is forming, where, who carries it, how early — with the evidence trail attached.
Independence matters

Ten posts from one publication are ten observations, but still only one source. Multiple channels from the same organization do not count as independent confirmation.

Source diversity

We combine editorial publications, independent creators, newsletters, video and community evidence rather than treating one platform as a proxy for culture. The composition of the source network is itself part of the method: signals confirmed across different kinds of source carry more weight than volume inside one kind.

Provenance

Every observation retains its original source, date and context. Analysis output is always labeled by its basis — full text, transcript, image and caption, or metadata only — so a conclusion can never look stronger than the material behind it.

Confidence and lifecycle

Signals carry visible confidence based on evidence strength, diversity and recency — never an unexplained percentage. Confidence is separate from lifecycle: a signal can be early and well-evidenced, or mainstream and thinly supported. Where the evidence does not support a judgment, the field is left blank rather than guessed.

Editorial judgment

Automation helps detect patterns. Cultural interpretation remains explicit, editable and open to contradiction. The platform distinguishes observed fact from inference, and inference from editorial judgment — and treats disagreement with its own earlier conclusions as information, not error.

Public digital behavior is evidence, not a complete representation of a population or city. Under-covered places may simply be under-observed. We would rather show you a small, honest number than a large, invented one.
