Method

Adopted: urban adoption score of 3 or more. Editorial-only: editorial momentum outruns adoption by two points or more (the † rule). Origin: the city named by a signal's earliest dated evidence. Blind spot: two or more signals present with no monitored local source. Uncorroborated: two or more signals present, every one resting on a single source. Under-sourced: three or more signals against at most one local source. All rules are computed, not asserted.
