Taste systems are forming

A taste system is a coherent combination of products, aesthetics, places, behaviors and references adopted together by a recognizable group: the unit brands actually need to see. Detecting one requires signals to repeatedly co-occur across independent evidence. The registry is young: what follows is the real connective tissue observed so far, not named systems.
